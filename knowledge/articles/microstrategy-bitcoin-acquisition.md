MicroStrategy has made a significant Bitcoin acquisition, purchasing 11,000 BTC for approximately $1.1 billion at an average price of $101,191 per Bitcoin. This latest purchase brings MicroStrategy's total Bitcoin holdings to 461,000 BTC, which were acquired for a total of approximately $29.3 billion at an average price of $63,610 per Bitcoin.

The company has achieved a BTC Yield of 1.69% year-to-date in 2025, demonstrating strong returns on its Bitcoin treasury strategy. This announcement reaffirms MicroStrategy's position as a major corporate holder of Bitcoin and reflects Michael Saylor's commitment to Bitcoin as a long-term store of value and treasury reserve asset.

## Key Takeaways

- MicroStrategy continues aggressive Bitcoin accumulation strategy
- Total holdings now represent 461,000 BTC with favorable cost basis
- Corporate treasury strategy focused on Bitcoin as inflation hedge
- Strong YTD 2025 returns indicate validation of Bitcoin investment thesis
