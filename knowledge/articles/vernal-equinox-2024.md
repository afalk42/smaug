NOAA's analysis of the vernal equinox timing and its historical significance. The 2024 vernal equinox occurred on March 19, marking a notable shift in equinox timing due to climate patterns and Earth's axial mechanics.

## Key Takeaways

- The 2024 vernal equinox on March 19 is among the earliest in modern records
- The last occurrence this early was in 1896, indicating a 128-year gap
- Equinox timing changes relate to Earth's orbital mechanics and climate factors
- Equal day/night length occurs across the entire planet on the equinox date
- Understanding equinox timing helps track long-term climate patterns
