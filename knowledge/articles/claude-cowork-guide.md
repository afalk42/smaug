A free interactive course introducing Claude Cowork, making AI assistance accessible to non-developers through a user-friendly GUI interface.

## Key Takeaways

- Claude Cowork is Claude Code for non-developers, offering file management and knowledge work capabilities in a nicer interface
- The course includes 12 hands-on lessons covering file organization, research synthesis, Excel/PowerPoint creation, and more
- 100% free with no gatekeeping or required signups

## Course Content

Hands-on lessons teaching:
- File organization and management
- Research synthesis and compilation
- Excel spreadsheet creation with working formulas
- PowerPoint presentation building
- Word document creation with proper formatting
- Knowledge management and synthesis
