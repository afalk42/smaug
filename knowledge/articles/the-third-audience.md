Dries Buytaert made his website easier for AI agents and crawlers to read by serving Markdown versions of pages, and within an hour was receiving hundreds of requests from ClaudeBot, GPTBot, and OpenAI's SearchBot. For two decades, the web was built for two audiences: humans and search engines. AI agents are now the third audience, and most websites aren't optimized for them yet.

## Key Takeaways

- Content negotiation allows serving Markdown instead of HTML when requests include `Accept: text/markdown` headers
- "Markdown auto-discovery" using link tags with `rel="alternate"` enables crawlers to quickly find AI-friendly content versions
- The speed of adoption shows AI agents actively seek cleaner content formats
- The value exchange between creators and AI companies remains unsettled; making content more accessible to AI may accelerate content commodification
- Generative Engine Optimization (GEO) and Answer Engine Optimization (AEO) are emerging as counterparts to traditional SEO
