Andy Weir released exclusive bonus content for "The Martian" as a 10-year anniversary gift to readers. "Lost Sols" provides additional stories and content from the world of The Martian, celebrating a decade since the novel's initial publication.

## Key Takeaways

- Exclusive bonus content released to commemorate The Martian's 10-year milestone
- Personal thank you from Andy Weir to the readers who supported the book's success
- Additional stories set in The Martian universe
