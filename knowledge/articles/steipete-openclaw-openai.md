Peter Steinberger (creator of OpenClaw) announces he is joining OpenAI to work on bringing agents to everyone. OpenClaw will transition to an open foundation, remaining independent and open-source while continuing to develop.

## Key Takeaways

- Steinberger joins OpenAI to drive the next generation of personal agents
- OpenClaw becomes a foundation — open, independent, and community-driven
- The project continues under new governance while Steinberger works at OpenAI
