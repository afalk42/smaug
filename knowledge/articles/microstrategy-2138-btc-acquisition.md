MicroStrategy announced a significant quarterly Bitcoin acquisition, purchasing 2,138 BTC for approximately $209 million at an average price of $97,837 per Bitcoin. This addition brings the company's total Bitcoin holdings to 446,400 BTC, acquired for approximately $27.9 billion at an average price of $62,428 per Bitcoin.

The company achieved exceptional returns on its Bitcoin treasury strategy, with a 47.8% yield in the fourth quarter and 74.1% year-to-date return for 2024. These results underscore MicroStrategy's aggressive Bitcoin accumulation strategy under CEO Michael Saylor.

## Key Metrics

- **Recent Acquisition:** 2,138 BTC for ~$209M
- **Total Holdings:** 446,400 BTC
- **Average Cost Basis:** ~$62,428 per BTC
- **Q4 Yield:** 47.8%
- **YTD 2024 Yield:** 74.1%
