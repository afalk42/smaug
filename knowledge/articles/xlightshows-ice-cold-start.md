A humorous custom light show created for Cybertruck that cleverly imitates the sound and behavior of a traditional internal combustion engine struggling to cold start. The show is available for free download from XLightShows.io, allowing Cybertruck owners to display this entertaining animation on their vehicle's LED system.

## Key Features

- Parody of ICE engine cold start sounds and behavior
- Free download available
- Custom light show for Cybertruck
- Humorous take on EV vs traditional vehicle differences
