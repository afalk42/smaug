## Overview

Alex Stanczyk, who spent 8 years managing a physical gold fund based in Switzerland, makes a compelling case for why Bitcoin is superior to gold as a store of value. His journey began with skepticism and ended with conviction—driven by understanding Bitcoin's technological advantages and experiencing gold's practical limitations during the COVID-19 pandemic.

## Key Argument: Physical Delivery Problem

The core issue with gold: wealth preservation requires physical custody and delivery, which depends on human cooperation across a supply chain (vault managers, security, customs, flights). During COVID-19 lockdowns in Switzerland, Stanczyk realized he couldn't guarantee client redemptions—a critical failure in the gold business.

Bitcoin solves this: instant settlement, global transfer at the speed of light, no intermediaries, no permission required.

## The Bitcoin Advantages

1. **Instant Global Settlement** - Move any amount instantly across the world
2. **No Intermediaries** - Complete custody and control, no counterparty risk
3. **Absolutely Finite** - Only 21 million Bitcoin will ever exist (vs. unlimited gold supply)
4. **Verifiable & Uncounterfeit** - Cryptographic certainty vs. assay risk
5. **No Government Seizure** - Decentralized protocol impossible to shut down

## The $900 Trillion Reset

Stanczyk argues that $900 trillion in global assets are mispriced due to fiat inflation. Many assets (real estate, stocks, art, gold) are held primarily as inflation hedges, not for intrinsic value. As Bitcoin gains adoption as the new benchmark asset and "hurdle rate," a massive rebalancing will occur.

**The Math:**
- 60 million millionaires worldwide
- Only 21 million Bitcoin will ever exist
- Scarcity alone will drive repricing

He predicts successful entrepreneurs and businesses will increasingly ask: "Can our returns outpace Bitcoin?" If not, they'll sell and hold BTC instead.

## The Climbing Mount Stupid Journey

Stanczyk's personal arc through Bitcoin adoption:
- **2011:** First heard of Bitcoin, dismissed it (thought government would shut it down)
- **2017:** Bought at $1,000, sold at $18,000 thinking it was a bubble
- **2019:** Started deep research after realizing Bitcoin was still here
- **2020 (COVID):** Had the catalyzing realization about gold's delivery vulnerabilities
- **Result:** Moved from managing gold funds to Bitcoin advocacy at Swan Bitcoin

## Key Takeaways

- Gold's strength (eternal, indestructible) is also a weakness (requires physical movement)
- Bitcoin as "digital settlement" is revolutionary for wealth preservation
- The finite supply of Bitcoin (21M max) is humanity's first experience with absolute scarcity
- Watch for the repricing cascade as Bitcoin becomes the new global benchmark asset
- Fiat currencies will persist (governments won't give up control) but Bitcoin will be the foundation
