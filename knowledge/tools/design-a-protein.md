# Design a Protein

## Overview

An interactive web application that enables users to design novel proteins using AI in approximately 5 minutes without requiring any biology knowledge.

## Key Features

### Accessibility
- **Zero biology knowledge required** - simplified interface for non-scientists
- **5-minute design process** - rapid iteration from concept to design
- **Guided workflow** - step-by-step AI assistance throughout

### Validation & Application
- **Laboratory synthesis** - designs are actually synthesized in the lab
- **Functional testing** - 1000+ protein designs tested for real-world therapeutic applications
- **Iterative improvement** - real results inform future design iterations

## Design Process

The app walks users through:
1. Defining therapeutic target/function desired
2. AI generation of novel protein sequences
3. Visual design preview
4. Submission for lab validation

## Scientific Value

### Democratization of Protein Design

Traditional protein engineering requires:
- PhD-level biochemistry knowledge
- Years of laboratory experience
- Expensive computational infrastructure

This tool removes those barriers, enabling:
- Citizen scientists to contribute
- Rapid exploration of design space
- Mass validation of AI-generated proteins

### Therapeutic Development

The commitment to synthesize and test designs means:
- Real-world functional validation data collected
- Training dataset for future AI improvements
- Potential novel therapeutics discovered

## Potential Applications

- **Drug discovery** - design novel therapeutic proteins
- **Research** - generate protein variants for study
- **Education** - interactive learning tool for protein design
- **Biotech innovation** - accelerate protein engineering workflows

## Technical Approach

Uses AI (likely large language models or protein-specific neural networks) trained on:
- Known protein structures and functions
- Evolutionary sequence data
- Functional constraint patterns
